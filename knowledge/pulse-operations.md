# Pulse operations knowledge

## Anonymous survey controls

Identity tokens are separated from response content and configurable thresholds protect small groups.

Anonymous mode cannot be reversed by an administrator after collection begins.

## Questionnaire versioning

A live distribution is pinned to an immutable survey version.

Changes create a new version so question wording and response meaning remain auditable.

## Response quality signals

Completion time, duplicate tokens, straight-lining, and attention checks create review signals.

Signals never silently delete a response and exclusions are reported with analysis.

## Product boundary

Pulse does not supply paid respondent panels or claim statistical validity automatically. Sampling design, weighting, and interpretation remain research responsibilities.
